Unofficial immigration case tracker

Your cases, in view.

Case Orbit is an independent Chrome extension for checking public USCIS case-status information. Cases and cached results stay in your Chrome profile.

Private by design

Case Orbit has no publisher server, analytics, advertising, or account system. Receipt numbers are sent directly to the official USCIS website only when a check runs.
