Support

Help for installing, checking, and removing locally saved case information.

Install or reload

For a local build, open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the Case Orbit folder. After an update, choose Reload on the extension card.

Refresh a case

Open Case Orbit and use the refresh button. Automatic checks are Off by default; you can opt into 6, 12, or 24 hours while Chrome is running.

Browser verification

If USCIS requests verification, open egov.uscis.gov in a normal tab and follow its instructions, then retry later. Case Orbit does not solve or bypass CAPTCHAs and preserves the last successful result.

Delete saved data

Expand a case and click Delete case twice to delete it. To delete all Case Orbit data, uninstall the extension from Chrome. The extension stores its data in chrome.storage.local.

Contact

Email tape.us.app@gmail.com and include the extension version, Chrome version, and exact error text. Do not send a complete USCIS receipt number.

Case Orbit has no analytics and is not affiliated with USCIS or DHS. Information shown by the extension is not legal advice. Checks send receipt numbers only to egov.uscis.gov.
